# Artshio

> Where art meets trust.

Artshio turns provenance into a living record — authorship, custody, and ownership that anyone can verify. Artists, estates, collectors, and institutions share the same certified trail, from the studio to the secondary market.

Certification can be inspected without a wallet. Optional on-chain anchors and collector participation settle on the Stellar network. A participation is not legal title to the physical work; that distinction stays visible in the product.
